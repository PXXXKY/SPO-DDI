# Third-party components

SPO-DDI depends on the following research resources:

| Component | Role |
|---|---|
| MSFCL | graph-based backbone extended by SPO-DDI |
| TrimNet | structural molecular representation |
| IBM MoLFormer | frozen SMILES representation |
| DDInter | drug-drug interaction data source |

This repository does not vendor the complete upstream source repositories,
datasets, pretrained model weights, or checkpoints. Obtain those resources
from their original providers and follow their respective terms of use.

The repository-level license does not override third-party copyrights or data
licenses.
